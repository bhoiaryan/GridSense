\subsection{Modular Multi-Tier Architecture}
\label{subsec:architecture_overview}

\subsection{Core Architecture Layers}
\label{subsec:architecture_layers}

\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.5em, itemsep=0.25em]
    \item \textbf{Data Ingestion \& Validation Layer:} Automatically verifies input schema formats, imputes missing telemetry, aligns time series to standard UTC/local hourly intervals, and filters non-physical sensor anomalies.
    \item \textbf{Forecasting Engine:} Transforms validated weather and lag features into multi-step hourly solar/wind generation trajectories across 24h, 48h, and 72h horizons.
    \item \textbf{Uncertainty \& Risk Engine:} Generates probabilistic confidence intervals ($p_{10}, p_{50}, p_{90}$) and scans for surplus/shortfall imbalances and steep ramp rates.
    \item \textbf{Simulation \& Optimization Engine:} Executes what-if scenario perturbations and solves multi-variable dispatch trade-offs (battery storage, backup, import/export, curtailment).
    \item \textbf{Recommendation \& Copilot Layer:} Translates mathematical optimization results into structured, natural-language guidance with operational reasons and expected impacts.
    \item \textbf{Operator Dashboard:} Presents interactive generation curves, risk timelines, and what-if controls for human operator review and dispatch execution.
\end{itemize}
