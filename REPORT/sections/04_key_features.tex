\section{Key Features}
\label{sec:key_features}

GridSense AI consolidates seven high-value operational capabilities into an integrated control-room platform:

\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=1.8em, itemsep=0.55em]
    \item \textbf{Multi-Horizon Renewable Forecasting (24--72 Hours):} Predicts solar irradiance and wind power generation across 24h, 48h, and 72h horizons by ingesting numerical weather predictions, historical generation records, and site physical parameters.
    
    \item \textbf{Probabilistic Uncertainty Quantification:} Generates statistical prediction intervals ($p_{10}, p_{50}, p_{90}$) and confidence ribbons alongside point estimates, enabling operators to differentiate between stable atmospheric regimes and high-volatility transitions.
    
    \item \textbf{Automated Surplus and Shortfall Detection:} Continuously compares generation trajectories against scheduled demand baselines to detect upcoming supply deficits (shortfalls) and excess energy periods (surpluses) with advance warning.
    
    \item \textbf{Interactive What-If Scenario Sandbox:} Empowers control-room operators to perturb environmental conditions (e.g., +30\% cloud cover), demand spikes, or asset availability (e.g., lower battery SOC or generator outages) and immediately inspect updated forecasts and dispatch options.
    
    \item \textbf{Intelligent Action Recommendations:} Synthesizes candidate dispatch strategies---including Battery Energy Storage System (BESS) charging/discharging, inter-regional grid power transfers, backup unit commitment, and managed curtailment.
    
    \item \textbf{Explainable AI Decision Support:} Delivers natural-language justifications for every recommended action, explaining the meteorological drivers, risk factors, and expected operational impacts to eliminate black-box hesitation.
    
    \item \textbf{Cost and Carbon Optimization Intelligence:} Evaluates operational choices against marginal fuel costs of thermal backup, battery cycling degradation, and avoided carbon emissions, prioritizing zero-emission, low-cost mitigations.
\end{enumerate}

These capabilities operate cohesively under the guiding operational principle:
\begin{center}
    \textbf{\color{primaryNavy}FORECAST $\longrightarrow$ UNDERSTAND $\longrightarrow$ SIMULATE $\longrightarrow$ OPTIMIZE $\longrightarrow$ ACT}
\end{center}
