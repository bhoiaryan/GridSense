\section{Proposed Solution}

\subsection{What is GridSense AI?}
\label{subsec:what_is_gridsense}

\textbf{GridSense AI} is an AI-powered renewable energy intelligence and decision-support platform that transforms solar and wind generation forecasts into actionable, optimized grid operational decisions.

The platform ingests numerical weather feeds, historical plant generation records, and site-level physical parameters to predict renewable power generation across 24-hour, 48-hour, and 72-hour planning horizons. Rather than terminating at a passive generation chart, GridSense AI quantifies forecast uncertainty, detects upcoming surplus and deficit events, simulates what-if scenarios, and generates explainable dispatch recommendations.

\subsection{Core Distinction: Decision Support vs. Passive Forecasting}
\label{subsec:differentiation}

The fundamental innovation of GridSense AI is the paradigm shift from passive monitoring to active decision support:
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.5em, itemsep=0.25em]
    \item \textbf{Conventional Systems:} $\text{Data} \rightarrow \text{Forecast} \rightarrow \text{Passive Dashboard}$
    \item \textbf{GridSense AI:} $\text{Data} \rightarrow \text{Forecast} \rightarrow \text{Risk} \rightarrow \text{Decision} \rightarrow \text{Recommendation}$
\end{itemize}

\textbf{Responsible Decision-Support Positioning:} GridSense AI operates strictly as an advisory decision-support system. It assists qualified control-room operators with multi-variable optimizations and transparent justifications, while operators retain final authority over grid dispatch commands.
